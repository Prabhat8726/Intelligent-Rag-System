# Accounts Payable FAQ

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company FAQ.

## Q1. A vendor asks why their invoice has not been paid. What do I check first?

Open the invoice in the document platform. The review panel lists every reason the invoice is held: a missing purchase order, a price or quantity difference, a possible duplicate or a value the system could not read. Most delays are missing purchase orders or price differences without a change order.

## Q2. When are vendors paid?

In the weekly payment run every Thursday, for all approved invoices that are due before the next run.

## Q3. Can I approve a price increase on an invoice?

No. Price increases need an approved change order before the invoice date. Only the Procurement Manager may accept small price variances after the fact, within the limits of the Procurement Policy.

## Q4. The vendor sent the same invoice twice by e-mail. What happens?

The platform flags the second copy as a duplicate because the vendor and the invoice number match. Reject it with the reason "duplicate" so that it is never paid.

## Q5. Who can change a vendor's bank account?

A Finance employee enters the change after confirming it by calling a known contact at the vendor. A second Finance employee approves it. The same person can never enter and approve the change.

## Q6. The invoice is in a different currency from the purchase order. Can I convert it?

No. The invoice must be in the currency of the purchase order. Ask the vendor for a corrected invoice.

## Q7. How long do we keep invoices?

Ten years after the end of the fiscal year, as set out in the Records Retention policy.
