# Procurement and Accounts Payable Glossary

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - general reference written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.

## Change order

A formal amendment to an approved purchase order that changes its price, quantity, delivery date or terms. It is approved like a new purchase order for the new total value.

## Credit note

A document issued by a vendor that reduces the amount owed, for example after returned goods, a price correction or an over-billed quantity.

## Delivery note

The document that accompanies a delivery and lists the items and quantities shipped. The quantities confirmed on receipt are the basis for the delivered quantity in the three-way match. Also called a packing slip or goods received note.

## Incoterms

Standard trade terms published by the International Chamber of Commerce that define which party pays for transport and insurance and when the risk passes from seller to buyer, for example EXW, FCA or DDP.

## Purchase order

The buyer's approved order to a vendor, stating items, quantities, prices, delivery terms and payment terms. It is the reference against which invoices are matched.

## Three-way match

The comparison of an invoice with its purchase order and its delivery notes before payment: the company pays only for what it ordered, at the agreed price, and what it received.

## Two-way match

The comparison of an invoice with its purchase order only, used for services and for goods without a delivery note.

## Payment terms

The number of days after the invoice date within which an invoice must be paid, for example "net 30". Early-payment discounts such as "2/10 net 30" reduce the price if paid within the shorter period.
