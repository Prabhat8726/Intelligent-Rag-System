# Procurement Policy (2025 edition)

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company policy. This edition was replaced by the 2026 edition.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for buying goods and services at Meridian Manufacturing Co. It applies to every department and every purchase paid by the company.

## 2. Purchase orders

A purchase order is required for every purchase above 1,000 USD. Invoices should quote the purchase order number; invoices without one are matched manually by Accounts Payable.

## 3. Price variance

Invoiced unit prices may differ from the purchase order by up to 2 percent without further approval. Larger differences are referred to the requester.

## 4. Payment terms

The standard payment term is 45 days from the invoice date. Payment terms of up to 90 days may be agreed with strategic vendors by the Procurement Manager.

## 5. Duplicate invoices

Accounts Payable checks the invoice number before payment to avoid paying an invoice twice.
