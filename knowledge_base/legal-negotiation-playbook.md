# Legal Negotiation Playbook

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not real legal guidance. Restricted to the Legal department.

## 1. How to use this playbook

This playbook lists the company's preferred positions and the fallback positions Legal may accept when negotiating supplier contracts. Fallback positions are confidential and must not be shared with suppliers or with colleagues outside Legal.

## 2. Liability cap fallback

The preferred position is a liability cap of twice the annual contract value. Legal may accept a cap equal to the annual contract value for low-risk suppliers. A cap below the annual contract value is never accepted.

## 3. Termination fallback

The preferred notice for termination for convenience is 30 days. Legal may accept up to 90 days for suppliers that make significant up-front investments for the company.

## 4. Payment terms fallback

Legal supports Procurement in negotiating 60-day payment terms. For suppliers that are small businesses, Legal may agree to 30 days to comply with prompt-payment commitments.

## 5. Escalation

Positions outside this playbook are escalated to the General Counsel.
