# Approval Matrix and Delegation of Authority

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company policy.

## 1. Principles

Every commitment of company money needs an approval from a person with the authority for its total value. Approvals are given in the system of record, never by e-mail alone. The total value of a commitment includes tax, freight and all expected follow-up orders under the same agreement.

### 1.1 Maker-checker

The person who requests a purchase, enters an invoice or proposes a payment cannot approve it. A second person always approves. This also applies to changes of vendor bank details, which need an approver from Finance who did not enter the change.

### 1.2 Conflicts of interest

An approver who has a personal interest in the vendor or the purchase must hand the approval to their own manager, as required by the Compliance Policy.

## 2. Purchase approval limits

| Total value (USD) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| up to 5,000 | Department manager |
| 5,000 to 50,000 | Finance Manager |
| 50,000 to 250,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| above 250,000 | Chief Financial Officer and Chief Executive Officer |

Capital expenditure above 50,000 USD also needs a business case reviewed by Finance before approval.

## 3. Invoice exceptions

### 3.1 Discrepancies

An invoice held by the three-way match is released only when the discrepancy is resolved. Price variances are accepted by the Procurement Manager within the limits of the Procurement Policy; quantity variances are resolved by a credit note or a later delivery, never by an approval alone. Vendor mismatches are escalated to the Finance Manager.

### 3.2 Duplicate suspects

A possible duplicate invoice is released only after Accounts Payable has confirmed with the vendor that the invoices are different. A confirmed duplicate is rejected and the vendor is notified.

## 4. Payments

Payment runs are approved by the Finance Manager. Payments above 100,000 USD in a single transfer also need the approval of the Chief Financial Officer. Urgent payments outside the weekly payment run need the Finance Manager and a documented reason.

## 5. Emergency purchases

In an emergency that threatens safety or production, a manager may order goods up to 25,000 USD without a prior purchase order. The purchase order must be raised and approved within two business days after the order.
