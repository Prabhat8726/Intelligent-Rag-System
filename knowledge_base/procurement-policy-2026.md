# Procurement Policy

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for buying goods and services at Meridian Manufacturing Co. It applies to every department, every legal entity and every purchase paid by the company, whatever the amount or the payment method. Purchases made with a corporate card follow the Travel and Expense Policy instead.

The policy protects the company against paying for goods that were not ordered, not delivered or not priced as agreed. Accounts Payable enforces it when invoices are processed; the document intelligence platform checks most of the rules automatically and sends exceptions to the review queue.

## 2. Purchase orders

### 2.1 When a purchase order is required

A purchase order (PO) is required for every purchase of goods or services above 500 USD. The purchase order must be approved before the order is placed with the vendor, following the Approval Matrix. Splitting one purchase into several smaller orders to stay below an approval limit is not allowed.

### 2.2 No PO, no pay

Invoices must quote the purchase order number. An invoice without a purchase order reference is not paid until the requester provides the purchase order. Accounts Payable asks the requester for it and holds the invoice; the vendor is told to quote the number on future invoices.

### 2.3 Changing a purchase order

Prices, quantities and payment terms may only change through an approved change order. A change order is approved at the level that applies to the new total value of the purchase order.

## 3. Three-way match

Before an invoice is paid, it is compared with the purchase order and with the delivery notes (goods received) for that order. This is the three-way match.

- Every invoiced item must appear on the purchase order.
- The invoiced unit price must equal the price on the purchase order (see section 4).
- The invoiced quantity may not exceed the ordered quantity, and may not exceed the quantity recorded as delivered on the delivery notes for that order.
- The vendor, the currency and the tax rate on the invoice must match the purchase order.

Services without a delivery note are matched against the purchase order and a service acceptance confirmed by the requester.

## 4. Price variance

### 4.1 Tolerance

Invoiced unit prices must match the purchase order exactly. A difference of up to 0.01 in the invoice currency per unit is accepted to allow for rounding. Any larger difference is a price variance and the invoice is held.

### 4.2 Price increases

A vendor may not raise a price by invoicing it. A price increase is only accepted when a change order to the purchase order was approved before the invoice date. Price variances without a change order are rejected and the vendor is asked for a credit note or a corrected invoice.

### 4.3 Who can accept a price variance

Only the Procurement Manager may accept a price variance after the fact, up to 5 percent of the line value and within the approval limits of the Approval Matrix. Larger variances need a change order.

## 5. Quantity variance

Partial deliveries are allowed: an invoice may cover part of an order. The company never pays for more than was delivered. If the invoiced quantity is higher than the delivered quantity, the invoice is held until the missing goods are delivered or the vendor issues a credit note for the difference. Over-deliveries are returned at the vendor's cost unless the requester accepts them through a change order.

## 6. Vendors

### 6.1 Approved vendors only

Purchases may only be made from vendors in the vendor master. The invoice vendor must be the vendor on the purchase order; an invoice from a different company, including an affiliate of the vendor, is held and investigated.

### 6.2 New vendors

New vendors are onboarded by Procurement. Onboarding includes verification of the tax identification number, bank details confirmed by a call-back to a known contact, and sanctions screening as described in the Compliance Policy.

## 7. Payment terms

The standard payment term is 30 days from the invoice date. Payment terms longer than 60 days are not allowed without written approval of the Chief Financial Officer. Payment terms shorter than 30 days require the approval of the Finance Manager. Early-payment discounts are taken whenever the discount rate is above 1 percent.

## 8. Duplicate invoices

An invoice with the same vendor and the same invoice number as an invoice already received is a duplicate and is not paid. An invoice from the same vendor for the same amount within seven days of another invoice is treated as a possible duplicate and checked by Accounts Payable before payment.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval of the Chief Financial Officer and are recorded in the audit trail with the reason. Repeated exceptions for the same vendor are reported to the Audit Committee every quarter.
