# Contract Management Guidelines

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company guideline.

## 1. When a written contract is needed

A written contract is required for every supply or service relationship expected to exceed 25,000 USD per year, for any processing of personal data by a vendor, and for any work performed on company premises. Purchase orders under the general terms of purchase are sufficient below these thresholds.

## 2. Mandatory clauses

Every supplier contract must contain the following clauses. Contracts without them are sent back to the business owner before signature.

### 2.1 Term and termination

The contract states its start date, its end date or renewal mechanism, and the company's right to terminate for convenience with no more than 90 days notice. Termination for material breach must be possible after a cure period of no more than 30 days.

### 2.2 Limitation of liability

The supplier's liability cap may not be lower than the annual contract value. Liability for personal injury, fraud and breaches of confidentiality or data protection must not be capped.

### 2.3 Data protection

If the supplier processes personal data for the company, a data processing agreement is attached and the supplier commits to report personal data breaches within 48 hours.

### 2.4 Governing law

Contracts are governed by the law of the State of Ohio, with disputes heard in the courts of Columbus, unless Legal approves another jurisdiction.

## 3. Approval of contracts

Contracts are approved under the Approval Matrix based on their total value over the full term. Any deviation from the mandatory clauses also needs the approval of Legal.

## 4. Renewals and expiry

The contract owner reviews each contract 90 days before its renewal notice date. The document intelligence platform warns when a contract expires within 30 days or has already expired; an expired contract without renewal means purchases from that supplier need a new contract or an approved extension.

## 5. Contract versions

Every amended version of a contract is uploaded as a new version of the same document, so that added, removed and modified clauses can be compared. Signed versions are never overwritten.
