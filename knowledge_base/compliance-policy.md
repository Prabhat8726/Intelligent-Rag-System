# Compliance and Anti-Bribery Policy

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company policy.

## 1. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts and hospitality from vendors only when they are modest, infrequent and not linked to a decision. The limit is 100 USD per gift and 250 USD per vendor per year. Anything above the limit is declined or reported to Compliance within five business days. Cash and cash equivalents such as gift cards are never accepted.

## 2. Conflicts of interest

Employees declare any personal or financial interest in a vendor, a customer or a competitor. An employee with a conflict of interest does not take part in selecting, approving or paying that vendor. Declarations are renewed every year.

## 3. Sanctions screening

Every new vendor and every vendor whose name, owner or bank country changes is screened against the applicable sanctions lists before any payment. Payments to a vendor with an unresolved screening match are blocked.

## 4. Segregation of duties

No single person can create a vendor, approve a purchase, receive the goods and approve the payment. The system enforces maker-checker for purchases, invoices, payments and changes to vendor bank details.

## 5. Reporting concerns

Concerns about bribery, fraud or policy breaches are reported to Compliance or through the anonymous whistleblowing line. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is forbidden.
