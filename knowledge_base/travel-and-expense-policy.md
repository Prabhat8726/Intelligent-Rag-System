# Travel and Expense Policy

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the demo knowledge base of Meridian Manufacturing Co.; not a real company policy.

## 1. Booking travel

Business travel is booked through the company travel agency. Economy class is used for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotel rates up to 220 USD per night are accepted without further approval.

## 2. Expense claims

Expense claims are submitted within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed unless the Finance Manager approves an exception.

### 2.1 Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense above 25 USD. A receipt must show the merchant, the date, the items and the amount paid. Card slips without items are not sufficient. A missing receipt can be replaced once per quarter by a signed statement for an amount up to 75 USD.

### 2.2 Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day. Business meals with customers or vendors need the names of the participants and are subject to the gift and hospitality limits of the Compliance Policy.

## 3. Corporate cards

Corporate cards are used for travel and small purchases below 500 USD. They may not be used for purchases that require a purchase order. Card statements are reconciled monthly with the receipts.

## 4. Non-reimbursable expenses

Fines, personal entertainment, upgrades not allowed by this policy and expenses of accompanying family members are not reimbursed.
